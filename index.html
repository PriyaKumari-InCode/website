About PIES

PIES, BIT Sindri

Innovation • Technology • Excellence

Engineering Ideas Into Reality

The Production and Industrial Engineering Society (PIES) of BIT Sindri is a student-oriented technical society dedicated to promoting technical knowledge, innovation, and professional development in the fields of production and industrial engineering.

PIES provides students with opportunities to explore recent advancements in industrial management, manufacturing technologies, automation, robotics, additive manufacturing, and Industry 4.0.

Through technical events, seminars, quizzes, discussions, webinars, and collaborative activities, the society encourages students to connect classroom learning with real-world industrial applications.

Our goal is to cultivate creativity, problem-solving abilities, teamwork, and an innovative mindset that prepares students for the evolving engineering landscape.
